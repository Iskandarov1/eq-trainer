# Book-derived feature backlog (owner selection pending)

10 feature candidates designed from research source #6 — Daniel Goleman, *Emotional Intelligence* (PRD §6). Prepared 2026-07-30; the owner chose to ship only the 20 phrases for now. Nothing here is implemented until the owner picks.

1. **Stoplight Meta-Moment** (pp. 197, 201, 25–27, 109) — launchable red/yellow/green flow: Red = calm down (BreathingGuide `pattern:'three'`), Yellow = SOCS form (situation, options + consequences, positive goal), Green = commit; plus a "Rehearse" mode with rehearsal counter (responses must be overlearned in calm states to surface in crisis). Effort M. Upgrades roadmap #3.
2. **Anger Cool-Down Gate** (pp. 48–52, 191, 210) — intensity slider gates the anger practice: ≥7 → cool-down screen (wave timer, distraction menu, ventilation-fallacy explainer); adds a "What's underneath this anger?" picker (hurt/fear/shame/envy/powerlessness) cross-linking modules + a residual-arousal ("hard day already?") tag. Effort M.
3. **Worry Interrupt — Borkovec protocol** (pp. 53–55) — fear practice variant: personal early-cue list → 4-7-8 relaxation → four challenge questions (Probable? Only one outcome? Constructive steps? Does rehearsing help?); saves as fear entries (`kind:'worry'`). Effort M.
4. **Granular Check-In** (pp. 187, 191, 207–208) — daily 2-tap check-in: 1–10 score + emotion family → precise word (~8 words per family from Appendix A); low scores invite a line; sparkline + streak on Dashboard; optional "feelings cube" granularity drill. Effort M. Upgrades roadmap #1+#2.
5. **XYZ Complaint Composer** (pp. 101, 106, 109, 113–114) — "When you did X / I felt Y / I'd rather Z" with a contempt linter (always/never, character verdicts, insults — each explained) + saved phrasings library + receiving-criticism checklist. Effort M.
6. **Flooding Meter & 20-minute time-out** (pp. 103–104, 107, 170–171, 80) — one-time baseline via manual 15-second pulse count; in a hot moment, re-count: ≥10 bpm over baseline → flooding declared, 20-minute countdown (5 min isn't enough) + distraction suggestions + regulate-before-relate re-entry check. Fully manual, no sensors. Effort M.
7. **Fear Ladder** (pp. 160–161) — name a specific fear, define 3–7 rungs from trivially easy to the real thing; rungs unlock in order (journey path grammar); per-rung reflection saved as a fear entry. Framed by "temperament is not destiny". Effort M.
8. **Toxic-Thought Catcher** (pp. 102–103, 108, 126, 176, 67–69) — quick capture of the hostile/automatic thought verbatim → "Stop — it may not be true" beat → evidence against → most benign explanation (+ optimism reframe: wrong approach, not "I'm a failure"); the log makes recurring scripts visible. Effort S — cheapest high-value pick.
9. **Marshmallow Drill** (pp. 62–64, 8) — urge-delay gym on the Breathe page: name a live urge, pick a tactic (look away / self-talk / hands busy / sing / picture the goal), hold through an escalating countdown 60s → 90s → 2min → 5min; held/gave-in log per tactic ("your best tactic: self-talk, 8/10 holds"); badge at 10 holds. Effort M.
10. **"Now or Then?" Trigger Atlas** (pp. 23–24, 212) — optional field on every practice: "Was this about now, or about then?" + "This reminded me of…"; Journal gains a grouped Trigger Atlas view of "then"-tagged entries; one Learn card explains the mechanism with the p. 212 quote. Effort S. Upgrades roadmap #7.

**Pairing notes (from the PRD-agent scoring):** #2+#6 form a complete anger-physiology system; #3+#7 together are the book's full anxiety curriculum; #4 is the only pick creating a new daily habit loop; #8 and #10 are the two cheapest wins; #1's Yellow phase pre-empts roadmap #6 (fold or defer it).
